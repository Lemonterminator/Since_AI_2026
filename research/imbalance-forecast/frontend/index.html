    Methodology

    Vartti averages two independently trained forecasters. Both predict the imbalance price minus the spot price from the same 19 inputs and are trained on every label published before the forecast period; prices before June 2026 are recomputed under the current pricing rule.

    1. 1Inputs available at issue time

      Latest published imbalance price, spread to spot and regulation direction, mFRR activation, the spot price and Fingrid day-ahead wind and net-load forecasts of each delivery quarter, calendar, and a flag for the old pricing rule. Only rows published by issue time are used.

    2. 2Gradient-boosted quantiles

      LightGBM predicts 19 quantiles of the spread, sorted so they never cross, and the regulation direction. Exceptional price probabilities come from the same quantile distribution.

    3. 3Neural network ensemble

      Four architectures (TabularResNet, TiDE, Conv-BiGRU, Transformer-MoE), three seeds each, trained on the same inputs and labels.

    4. 4Equal-weight blend

      Quantiles and probabilities of the two are averaged. On the official validation window the blend has a P50 MAE of 41.4 €/MWh, against 66.2 for the persistence baseline.

    5. 5Forecast output

      Median, P10–P90 (an 80% range), direction probabilities and the probability of a price more than 50 €/MWh above or below spot.

    No look-ahead bias

    Each input row is used only from its first usable origin. The latest published imbalance price starts 75 minutes before issue time, shown as the hatched publication lag. Replay withholds later prices until actuals are shown.

    Uncertainty validation

    Track record compares forecast probabilities with observed frequencies. The reliability panel checks interval width, revision size, recent coverage and data latency using only information available at the viewing time.

    Transparent decision rules

    Forecast values come from the model output. Review items are fixed rules applied to them, so each item can be traced and challenged.

    Thresholds

    Alert and revision thresholds. Forecast values are unaffected. Stored locally in this browser.
